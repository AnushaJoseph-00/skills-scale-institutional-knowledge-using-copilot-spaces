# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with:** UX/UI Designer (on design specifications), Product Manager (on acceptance criteria), Scrum Master (on blockers), Data Analyst (on observability metrics), Support Engineer (on bug reproduction)

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with:** Project Manager (on timelines and dependencies), Developers (on feasibility), UX/UI Designer (on user needs), Data Analyst (on success metrics), Support Engineer (on customer feedback)

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with:** Product Manager (on priorities), Scrum Master (on agile ceremonies), Developers (on estimates and blockers), All roles (on escalation and coordination)

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on continuous improvement. They act as servants to the team and champions of the Scrum process while working closely with the Project Manager to ensure smooth delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on Scrum practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain sprint board and burndown charts
- Shield the team from external distractions and scope creep

### Goals
- Enable high team velocity and autonomy
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Reduce process friction and cycle time

### Typical Communication
- Agile ceremonies (standups, planning, retros)
- One-on-ones with team members to identify blockers
- Metrics and retrospective action items

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with:** Project Manager (on escalations and timelines), Developers (on impediments and process improvement), Product Manager (on sprint readiness), All roles (on retrospective insights)

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers own the user experience and interface design. They conduct user research, create prototypes, and collaborate with Developers and Product Managers to ensure solutions are user-centric, accessible, and delightful.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design user interfaces and interaction flows
- Create prototypes and design specifications
- Ensure accessibility standards are met (WCAG, etc.)
- Participate in design reviews and iterate based on feedback
- Collaborate on user acceptance testing

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce support burden through thoughtful design
- Validate design decisions with real user feedback

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design specifications and prototypes
- Collaboration with Developers during implementation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with:** Product Manager (on user needs and requirements), Developers (on feasibility and implementation), Support Engineer (on user pain points), QA/Testing (on usability acceptance criteria)

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts translate business questions into data analysis, measure project impact, and provide actionable insights. They support Product Managers in defining success metrics and help teams make data-driven decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track success metrics aligned with project goals
- Analyze project impact using quantitative data
- Build dashboards and reports for stakeholders
- Identify data quality issues and gaps
- Provide insights to inform prioritization and iteration
- Support Developers and QA with observability and analytics setup

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making
- Provide clear visibility into project ROI and impact
- Identify improvement opportunities through data

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metrics and dashboard reviews
- Analysis reports and insights shared with stakeholders
- Meetings with Product Manager on success metric tracking
- Technical discussions with Developers on instrumentation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with:** Product Manager (on success metrics definition), Project Manager (on KPI tracking), Developers (on instrumentation), Support Engineer (on usage patterns and trends)

---

## Support Engineer

### Role Summary
Support Engineers handle post-release issues, triage customer-reported bugs, and bridge communication between users and the development team. They provide critical feedback on product stability and user pain points.

### Responsibilities
- Triage and document customer-reported issues
- Reproduce bugs and provide detailed reports to Developers
- Escalate critical production issues
- Provide first-level support and workarounds
- Gather customer feedback and usage patterns
- Contribute to knowledge base and documentation

### Goals
- Resolve customer issues quickly and effectively
- Reduce support burden through documentation and preventive measures
- Provide voice-of-customer feedback to the product team

### Typical Communication
- Bug reports and issue tickets with reproduction steps
- Regular syncs with Developers on top issues
- Customer feedback summaries to Product Manager
- Escalation communications for critical incidents

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with:** Developers (on bug reproduction and resolution), Product Manager (on customer feedback and pain points), Project Manager (on incident escalation), Data Analyst (on usage and trend analysis)
